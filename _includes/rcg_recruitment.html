Chapter i

The Yard & the House

❦

The front patio is framed by a tangerine tree, a eucalyptus, and a forty-foot cedar. Behind them stands the house: two stories, five bedrooms, two baths. Out front there's a steel rack for bikes; inside, a free washer and dryer and a five-burner gas range that gets plenty of use.

We're adding raised garden beds soon, so some of what we eat can grow right on the lot. We're also setting up a communal food share, entirely optional, sourcing from Eatwell Farms, Trader Joe's, Costco, and the Berkeley Bowl.

Every tenant is eligible for a city parking permit, and there's ample space to park in the neighborhood.

Chapter iv

The People & the Block

❦

Over the last four years or so, our house has grown from a MechE hub into a home for humanities, neuroscience and meditation heads, culinary people, and young professionals. It's an ideal place for grad students and other young professionals who want to stay connected to local events and the things happening in the Bay.

We share responsibility for the house: cleaning, supplies, small projects, and the decisions that affect everyone. We look for people who communicate clearly and take initiative.

  • Quiet hours from 10:30 PM to 7:30 AM
  • Shared chores and the occasional house meeting
  • No pets; assistance animals, including ESAs, are accommodated as required by law

We're a block north of University Avenue, between Grant Street and McGee Avenue. UC Berkeley is several minutes away, Ohlone Park is a block over, and BART, the bus lines, Trader Joe's, and the Berkeley Farmers' Market are all a short walk.

Chapter v

How to Join

❦

Five short steps, so that we and you can both be sure.

  1. iApplyFill out a short form: who you are, what draws you here, and when you'd move in.
  2. iiOne-on-oneA relaxed conversation with one housemate, over coffee or a call.
  3. iiiHouse tourCome by in person. See the room, the kitchen, the yard, and the block.
  4. ivGroup interviewMeet the whole house together. Ask us anything; we'll do the same.
  5. vDeposit & offerIf it's a mutual fit, we'll make an offer. Sign the agreement; a $940 deposit holds your room.

…and then, welcome home.

The fine print: agreement & deposit

We'll share the full agreement for review before you commit. This preview is informational; the signed agreement sets out the final terms.

  • Rent: $940 per month, plus shared utilities estimated at $90–130 per month.
  • Deposit: $940, due after acceptance and signing. It confirms your commitment to move in and doubles as your security deposit.
  • Return: The deposit is held through the tenancy and returned after move-out at the end of your lease, less any lawful deductions (such as damage beyond ordinary wear and tear), subject to applicable law.
  • Shared expectations: Respect quiet hours, take part in shared upkeep, and communicate with housemates.

We'll confirm lease duration, payment logistics, and other terms in writing before any money is requested.
