Roommate Agreement

Term
January 1, 2027 – July 31, 2027
Renewal
Ongoing. The agreement is refreshed year to year on August 1, starting August 1, 2027.
Roommates
Five housemates (names withheld here)

This agreement is meant to make expectations clear so we can all live together smoothly and avoid misunderstandings.

1.Rent

Total rent for the house is $4,700 per month. Each roommate pays $940 per month.

Rent is due 72 hours before the end of each month to one of two designated housemates, who then transfer the rent to the landlord.

If one roommate is late, they're responsible for resolving it quickly. If late fees result from an individual delay, that roommate covers them.

2.Utilities

Utilities will be split evenly by default.

If there is known disproportionate usage, a different split may be agreed to in advance, or discussed and calculated after the month, with input from all housemates.

This includes (but isn't limited to): electricity, gas, water, internet, trash.

Utilities will be totaled and shared monthly. If a roommate fails to pay amounts owed under this agreement, they remain responsible for those amounts, and the other roommates may seek reimbursement.

Payment is due by the end of the month to whomever paid for utilities.

3.Security Deposit

Each roommate has contributed $940 toward the security deposit.

For a new roommate, the $940 is a holding fee and security deposit, and it is non-refundable. It is binding once paid: it holds the room, and it is not returned if the new roommate changes their mind.

The deposit is returned only upon move-out, subject to the deductions below.

When we move out, the landlord's deposit return (minus any deductions) will be split evenly unless damage is clearly attributable to a specific person.

If damage is clearly caused by one roommate, that roommate is responsible for the cost.

If a roommate moves out and leaves an unreasonable amount of trash or debris in the room or the house, a $200 fine may be deducted from their deposit.

4.Early Move-Out

This agreement assumes everyone plans to stay through July 31, 2027.

If someone needs to move out early, they must give two months advance notice. Otherwise, a penalty may be deducted from their deposit at the other housemates' discretion.

5.House Agreements, Communication & Good Faith

Shared expectations around use of common spaces, guests, noise, cleanliness, and communication are documented in the House Consensuses document. All roommates agree to follow those shared norms and to raise concerns early and directly when something feels off.

This agreement is entered into in good faith, with the shared goal of maintaining a respectful, supportive, and low-stress living environment.
