**We are in a crisis.** Super-intelligence is extremely near. Mythos surprised us all. Benchmarks are having trouble keeping up.

**Our politics is not ready.** Trump has wavered on AI safety policy, and so have Democrats like Gavin Newsom. Even if they were 100% in agreement, would we still be able to handle it? Think of how the US Government handled COVID-19.

**Change in politics comes from people.** It was a conservative president that passed the Civil Rights Act. Why? Because there was a movement.

**The populist movement is happening regardless.** The question is – are we able to use it to build a better future? In this, mainstream AI reform advocates and grassroots activists must work together.

**Ideological discomfort is essential for a broad coalition.** Alexei Navalny worked with white supremacists to fight Putin.

**Democratize AI.** AI by the people, for the people. That is something anyone — Democrat or Republican — can get behind.
