Not much changes in Sarasota, Florida, and I rather like it that way. My parents re-tiled the floor in our living room. There's a fife in my bedroom which I bought in Maryland one year when we visited the States. Scruffy the hedgehog sits on my bedside cupboard. 

Florida is cool, always humid. It's a real treat to be away from Berkeley, in a different headspace. It's also great to be in peace and quiet. Working from home out of my bedroom in Berkeley, it's hard to catch a break. Humans are creatures of habit, and my habit in my room is to think about the movement -- which can be a lot sometimes!

I've changed my website a little bit. It's looking nicer, but I'm not 100% sure of the direction I want it to go. And that's okay, it'll get better over time. Here in Florida, it feels like there is all the time in the world, and the AI crisis is far, far away.
